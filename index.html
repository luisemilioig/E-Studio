About the Studio

Artistry meets
Strategy.

E Studio is a creative studio founded by Luis E. — a visual artist and designer passionate about crafting intentional, thoughtful, and visually compelling brand experiences. Every project tells a story and connects deeply with its audience.

Our work reflects a fusion of modern minimalism and personal artistic expression. From branded visuals to experiential design, every element is thoughtfully crafted, every detail intentional. Collaborations with brands like Puma, Hoka, JD Sports, and Equinix — and features in print — speak to the reach of that work.
